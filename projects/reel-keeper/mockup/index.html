Reel Keeper · design mockup

Every reel you share, sorted and searchable.

Share a reel to Reel Keeper and it writes the summary, pulls out every website, app and place the video mentions, and files it into a category. This is a clickable mockup of the phone app, filled with sample reels.

Things to try

  1. Share a reel from the pretend Instagram screen and watch it get processed.
  2. Open a category, then a reel. The highlighted items are the links it found.
  3. Tap a reel's category to move it. The app would learn from that.
  4. Search for flashcards, porto or garlic. It searches what was said in the video, too.
  5. Open the Links tab to see every website and place in one list.

All reels, links and creators shown are sample content for the mockup. Nothing here is connected to Instagram.
